##### Backgroundstory

Hello, me Gimble. 

And this, is my story. 

I was Born in a cave but raised in the orphanage of Elderberry. 
Until Inzala ripped everything to pieces. But for me this was a good thing to happen, because of this i went on a journey.

In the swamps of Far Far and Further Away. You could find my two loving parents. Just normal gnomes with no magical powers at all. My magic came from somewhere else... 

When my mother Gisempa, who was pregnant with me, stumbled upon an old, abandoned cave, she decided it was time for her to rest. During labor, she passed away.
It's kind of ironic if you think about it, she gave me the gift of life, by paying it with hers.

This cave became my home untill both my father and mother died at the eternal age of 380 years old... 
Yes that is an accurate number, I suppose... 

Father could not bare living without my mother anymore. After all those years of spending together, fighting the nature for survival and building a family. He went to the forest in the search of food to nurture me, which he always did. Unfortunatly, after weeks of not showing up at the cave. I went out to find him and eventually I did... In pieces. Ripped body parts all over the place but I could tell it was my dad. 

Possibly angered a goblin or lost a fight from the unknown magical inhabitants of the forest. 
Anyhow... Back to my story.
The cave once called home was an old old, possibly ancient, cave where the Mighty Druids used to make sacrifices. 

While crying after my fathers death over a round stone. It lit up and covered me in light! Once I had brown eyes but after this event, they turned white. Covering me in magical powers which me discovered quickly after that. 

The other gnomes, in some other parts around the forest and cave, started acting very hostile towards me. I was still young and could not figure out why. The Elder gnome told me that it was written in the Old Tales that a gnome with white eyes was to become a legend. 
But sacrifices had to be made, a solo journey was upon me. Me as a youngster still, is looking for answers and where the roads will take me. I took initiative to protect the race and grabbed all of me belongings, skidaddling out of the cave, through the swamps into the forest. 
My first stop was Elderberry, wich did me well.
For some time i stayed there, under mother Thara her wings.
Gimble has yet to discover everything...
